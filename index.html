Business Recommendations

1. Tighten underwriting for high-risk pockets

Apply stricter approval rules or risk-based pricing to Grade D/E applicants with DTI above 40% (see risk pockets).
Evidence: Credit grade is the strongest risk driver: Grade E defaults at 13.0% vs 1.0% for Grade A (13.3x). Debt-to-income matters: borrowers at DTI 40%+ default at 5.8% vs 1.9% below 20%.

2. Add a utilisation trigger

Send applicants above 80% credit utilisation for secondary review before approval.
Evidence: Credit utilisation above 80% is a warning sign: 6.2% default vs 3.1% under 30%.

3. Staff ahead of the Q4 peak

Increase processing capacity from September and use monthly demand as a staffing forecast.
Evidence: Demand peaks in Oct, Nov, Dec (festive/year-end season) and is lowest in Feb: plan staffing and credit-check capacity ahead of Q4. SLA breaches (decision > 5 days) fell from 31.2% in 2023 to 20.4% in 2025, but remain material.
Data notes: the dataset is fully simulated for portfolio and analytical demonstration. Loss estimate assumes 60% loss given default. SLA breach = decision took more than 5 days. Recommendations reflect the full 2023-2025 portfolio.
